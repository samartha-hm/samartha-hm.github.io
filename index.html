WHO I AM

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate from Srinivas Institute of Technology, Valachil, Mangaluru (VTU, CGPA: 7.79 / 10), driven by a genuine curiosity about how complex systems are controlled, connected, and built.

I also actively integrate LLMs and AI coding agents into engineering research, rapid prototyping, and firmware debugging.

Beyond engineering, I create video and graphic content. I run DreamBeatz, a YouTube channel with 3K+ subscribers focused on music and lyrical edits.

  • Offline Vision Pipeline: Evaluated a separate offline vision pipeline on 4,000 images using U-Net segmentation (Dice coefficient > 0.92) and ResNet-50 classification: 96.5% accuracy for four-stage grading of unpeeled nuts and 98.2% for dry/not-dry classification of peeled nuts (TensorFlow/Keras).
